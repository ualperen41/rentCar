Vehicle Character & Aerodynamics

The 2024 Porsche 911 GT3 RS does not merely push mechanical boundaries; it abolishes them. Driven by a pure, naturally aspirated 4.0-liter flat-six that shrieks to a dizzying 9,000 RPM, this machine channels factory racing pedigree straight to sovereign asphalt. The crowning glory of the Weissach package delivers weight reduction via forged magnesium lightweight wheels, carbon fiber reinforced plastic (CFRP) stabilizers, and exposed carbon weaving across the hood, roof, and dynamic aero components.

Its Formula 1-derived active Drag Reduction System (DRS) articulates the monstrous swan-neck rear wing in fractions of a second, producing an unprecedented 1,895 lbs of downforce at 177 mph. Despite its relentless track focus, our curated executive model is equipped with front-axle lift geometry, active cruise moderation, high-fidelity BOSE® Surround Sound, and adaptive LED matrix illumination for effortless metropolitan transfers.
